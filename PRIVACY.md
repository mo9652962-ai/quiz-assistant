# Privacy Policy for quiz-assistant

**Last Updated:** October 2026

`quiz-assistant` is an open-source, local-first CLI tool for question-bank study and spaced repetition.

## 1. Local Processing & Zero External Telemetry
- All question records, study histories, and performance metrics are stored on local SQLite databases on your device.
- Zero analytics, trackers, or telemetry beacons exist in the codebase.

## 2. Data Ownership
- The user retains complete ownership of all imported question materials and learning history.
